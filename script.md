# Tadcu — Episode 1: "The Great Sheep Escape"

*Series: Tadcu · Episode 1 · Production: test01*

---

## Cold open — TITLE CARD (0:00–0:06)

Over rolling hills at dawn, hand-lettered title:

> **TADCU**
> *in "The Great Sheep Escape"*
>
> Subtitle: *Ymlaen!*

### SCENE 1 — THE COTTAGE (0:06–0:20)

**EXT. BWTHYN TADCU — MORNING**

Sun climbs over the hill. Smoke curls from the chimney of a stone cottage.

- **NARRATOR:** "Bore da, Cwm Twp. Grandad's favourite time of day: two boiled
  eggs, one cup of tea, and ninety-nine ewes — allegedly — in the right field."
- **TADCU** (sipping tea, at the gate): "Bore da, Bryn. And where — exactly —
  did I leave the girls last night?"
- **BRYN** (looks away, whistles casually).

Shot list:
1. WS — Dawn valley, chimney smoke drifting.
2. CU — Tadcu and the eternal thermos, raising the mug.
3. MS — Bryn beside the gate. The gate swings, an empty field beyond it.

### SCENE 2 — DISCOVERY (0:20–0:34)

**EXT. THE EMPTY FIELD**

- **TADCU:** "...Bryn."
- **BRYN:** (one slow, guilty tail-wag)
- **TADCU:** "The girls are OUT. Ninety-nine festivals of fluff, heading for
  the A-road and the chippie queue."
- **BRYN:** "Wrrf!" (Bryn takes charge)

Beat: Tadcu lowers his cap, grips his stick, and sets off at "a pace he
would describe as jogging."

### SCENE 3 — THE CHASE (0:34–0:50)

**EXT. THE HILLSIDE — CONTINUOUS**

The leading flock bounces downhill; Tadcu follows; Bryn overtakes them all.

Gags, in order:
1. Sheep take the wall — Tadcu takes the gate — the gate doesn't latch.
2. Tadcu skids on wet slate into a wheelbarrow of prize leeks. Leeks everywhere.
3. **TADCU:** "Diolch for your patience, girls! Your obedience is noted,
   generous in parts, and worthless!"
4. One sheep (Dott, the spotty one) stops dead on a boulder, chewing
   thoughtfully, like a mountain-goat statement piece.

### SCENE 4 — THE TURNING POINT (0:50–1:05)

**EXT. THE HILLSIDE**

Tadcu stops. Puts the stick down. Out of the cardigan pocket comes the
thermos — and a brown paper bag. The hill goes quiet.

- **TADCU** (quietly, to the hillside): "Teisen lap. Fresh off the griddle
  this morning, mind. Butter. Sugar. No nonsense."

A hundred noses lift on the wind. The flock freezes. Dott blinks first.

- **BRYN:** *single, professional bark.*
- **TADCU:** "Diolch, Bryn. And girls — gently on the banking, mind the
  bramble. There's mash in it for everybody."

### SCENE 5 — HOME (1:05–1:20)

**EXT. THE COTTAGE — EVENING**

The flock files back through the gate in single file. Tadcu counts them
past, muttering, always stuck around ninety.

- **TADCU** (counting): "Eighty-nine. Ninety. ...Eighty-nine? Whatever.
  Dott, we'll call it ninety for the road."
- **NARRATOR:** "Ninety-nine was the exact number. Tadcu never counted
  right in his life — he counts slow, like the seasons."
- **TADCU** (raises mug to camera): "Nos da, chi gyd. See you Tuesday —
  and the gate goes on the latch from now on." (winks)

**THE END.**

> *Next episode: Tadcu disagrees with the Post Office about a parcel that
> contains another parcel. Ymlaen!*

*Nos da.*

## Storyboard notes

- **Palette** — hill greens `#2f6a3c`, `#4a8a52`; cream `#f7e9d7`; slate
  `#5b6670`; Tadcu's cap and cardigan in brick red `#b04a3a`; sheep cream
  with charcoal-black faces and legs.
- **Shape language** — Sheep are clouds with legs; Tadcu is a teapot with a cap.
- **Timing** — The chase runs on cartoon-bounce timing; the other scenes
  breathe with long ease-in-outs.
- **Flicker rule** — nothing moves twice the same way; every sheep bobs on
  a different phase so the flock fizzes.
- **Source of truth** — `index.html`. The script above describes it, but the
  pixels in the browser win any argument.
