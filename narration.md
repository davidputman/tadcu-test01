# Tadcu — Episode 1: "The Great Sheep Escape"
## Narration (voice-over) script — recording copy

*For one narrator. Gentle, warm Welsh storytelling voice — unhurried, amused,
never shouting. Reads like a grandfather recounting it from an armchair by the
fire. Music bed: soft harp and low strings, dropping out for the chase.*

Character lines are included underneath each beat in *italics* for the
editor only — they stay as on-screen subtitle bubbles. **BOLD = narrator,
to be recorded.**

---

### ⏱ 0:00 — TITLE CARD (scene 1, 0:00–0:05.5)

**(NARRATOR, over the dawn hills)**
> **Shwmae — and welcome to Cwm Twp. A valley so quiet, you can hear the
> tea going cold.**

*Screen: on-screen line “Tadcu: Shwmae! Cap, stick, thermos…” ¹ is subtitle
only — the narrator opens over it.*

---

### ⏱ 0:05.5 — THE COTTAGE (scene 2)

**0:05.9**
> **Bore da, Cwm Twp. Grandad's favourite time of day: two boiled eggs,
> one cup of tea — and ninety-nine ewes, allegedly, in the right field.**

**0:14.0 (after Tadcu's line "Bore da, Bryn… and where exactly did I leave the
girls last night?")**
> **Note the gate, now. Look at it. Note it.**
> **Because in a moment — it matters.**

*Bryn whistles. Beat of silence. Harp only.*

---

### ⏱ 0:19.5 — THE EMPTY FIELD (scene 3)

**0:20.5 (after “…Bryn.”)**
> **An empty field. A guilty dog. And a trail of fluff, going over
> the hill into next door's parish.**

*(after Tadcu: “Ninety-nine festivals of fluff, heading for the A-road and
the chippie queue.” — Bryn: “Wrrf!”)*

**0:30.3**
> **He sets off at a pace he would describe as "jogging"
> — which is Welsh for "walking, but angrier".**

---

### ⏱ 0:33.5 — THE CHASE (scene 4)

**0:34.1**
> **Across the low pasture at a speed unrivalled since 1974 —
> the girls taking the wall, Tadcu taking the gate,
> and the gate taking no responsibility whatsoever.**

*(after Tadcu: “Your obedience is noted — generous in parts, and worthless!”)*

**0:43.1**
> **A skid! A wheelbarrow! Prize leeks — everywhere.
> The county show will hear about this.**

**0:45.5**
> **And one sheep — Dott, the clever one — stops on a boulder.
> Chews. Wins.**

---

### ⏱ 0:49.5 — TEISEN LAP TRUCE (scene 5)

**0:50.3**
> **And then Tadcu does the bravest thing a hill farmer can do.
> He stops.**

*(after Tadcu, quietly: “Teisen lap. Fresh off the griddle, mind. Butter.
Sugar. No nonsense.”)*

**0:59.7**
> **A warm smell on the wind. A hundred little ears
> lift like aerials. The hill holds its breath.**

*(Bryn: “WOOF.” The flock freezes.)*

**1:01.5**
> **Dott blinks first. Welsh cakes: one, nil.**

---

### ⏱ 1:04.5 — HOME (scene 6)

*(Sheep file back through the gate as Tadcu counts.)*

**1:05.3**
> **Ninety-nine ewes, filed back through the gate — counted by Tadcu,
> who counts slow, like the seasons, and lands on "enough" every time.**

*(after Tadcu: “Eighty–nine. Ninety… eighty–nine? Whatever. Dott — we'll
call it ninety for the road.”)*

**1:10.7**
> **Ninety-nine was the exact number. But you didn't hear that from me.**

*(after Tadcu: “Nos da, chi gyd! The gate goes on the latch from now on.”)*

**1:14.0**
> **The latch would last four days.**
> **But that — is next week's story.**

---

### ⏱ 1:19.5 — END CARD (scene 7)

**1:21.7 (as the sheep bob and "Nos da." appears)**
> **Nos da, Cwm Twp. Sleep well, mind the gate.**

*MUSIC OUT.*

## Recording notes

- **Runtime:** ~86 seconds total; ~9 lines of narration, ≈180 words.
- **Pace:** every line lands before its scene's comic beat, and then gets
  out of the way. Never read over a bubble.
- **Tone markers:** "allegedly", "which is Welsh for…", and "you didn't hear
  that from me" are wink lines — lean on them, don't rush them.
- **The chase (0:33–0:49)** is the only energetic passage; pick up the tempo
  there, then drop straight back to armchair for the truce.
- **Final line (“Sleep well, mind the gate”)** said dry, knowing the gate
  will lose again on Tuesday.
