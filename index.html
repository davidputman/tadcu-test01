Narrator Bore da, Cwm Twp. Two boiled eggs, one cup of tea — and ninety-nine ewes, allegedly, in the right field.
Tadcu Bore da, Bryn! And where — exactly — did I leave the girls last night?
Bryn (one slow, guilty tail-wag)
Tadcu ...Bryn.
Tadcu The girls are OUT. Ninety-nine festivals of fluff, heading for the A-road and the chippie queue.
Bryn Wrrf!
Narrator He sets off at a pace he would describe as “jogging”.
Narrator Across the low pasture, at a speed unrivalled since 1974.
Tadcu Your obedience is noted, girls — generous in parts, and worthless!
Dott (stops on the boulder. Chews. Wins.)
Narrator And then Tadcu stops. And the hill, sensibly, stops with him.
Tadcu (quietly) Teisen lap. Fresh off the griddle this morning, mind. Butter. Sugar. No nonsense.
Bryn WOOF.
Flock (one hundred noses lift on the wind)
Tadcu Eighty-nine. Ninety. ...Eighty-nine? Whatever. Dott — we’ll call it ninety for the road.
Narrator Ninety-nine was the exact number. Tadcu counts slow — like the seasons.
Tadcu Nos da, chi gyd! And the gate goes on the latch from now on.
